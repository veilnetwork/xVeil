xVeil's bundled country-to-CIDR routing table is generated from IPdeny country
IP blocks: https://www.ipdeny.com/ipblocks/

IPdeny permits redistribution and use of its generated country zone files.
The data is approximate, may become stale, and must not be treated as proof of
a person's physical location. Run `tool/update_geoip_routes.py` to refresh it.
